## Title

World Capitals Quiz – Countries & Maps

## Toolbar name (PluginConfig.json `name`)

World Capitals Quiz

## Short description (PluginConfig.json `desc`)

Handwritten quiz on world capitals and countries: worksheets, map quizzes and 2–4 player games, graded automatically on your Supernote.

## Description

Practise world capitals and countries with your pen. The plugin creates a
worksheet on the current note page, you write the answers by hand, and it
grades the sheet with Supernote's handwriting recognition.

Features
- Capitals worksheets: write the capital of each country, then tap Grade. Each answer gets a hand-drawn check, "almost" or cross mark, the recognised text and a total score.
- Map quizzes: mark capitals or countries with a cross on a high-contrast map made for e-ink. Capitals are scored by how many kilometres you were off.
- Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Oceania or the whole world.
- Choose 10, 15, 20 or all countries. Long sheets are spread over several pages.
- Accepts common alternative spellings and the English name of the capital.
- Multiplayer for 2–4 players on one device, for both worksheets and maps:
  - Pass around: each player gets their own pages with the same countries, with a hand-over page in between. Grade the whole set to see who won.
  - Take turns: one shared sheet where players answer in turn, each with their own countries. Points per player are shown at the bottom.
- Fix your answers and grade again as many times as you like.
- 55 languages, following the device language or chosen in the menu.
- Works fully offline. No data leaves the device.

Tip: use a separate notebook
Create a notebook just for the quiz (for example "Capitals"), with a blank
template. The plugin writes onto the page you are on and can add pages, so a
separate notebook keeps your other notes untouched and makes it easy to keep
or delete old quizzes. Each finished sheet also adds some size to the notebook.

How to use
1. Open a blank page in a notebook.
2. Tap World Capitals Quiz in the toolbar, choose continent, sheet type, number and players, and make the sheet.
3. Write your answers.
4. Open the menu again and tap Grade.

Permissions: reads and writes the current note page (to create and grade the sheet).

Data: country names from Unicode CLDR, capitals from Wikidata (CC0), maps from
Natural Earth (public domain). Maps show de facto boundaries and take no
position on disputed territories. Open source under the MIT License.
