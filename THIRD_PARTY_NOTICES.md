## Wikidata – capital names and coordinates

Capital names in all languages (`scripts/data/capitals-wikidata.json`, and
`scripts/data/co-capitals-wikidata.json` for de facto co-capitals) and capital
coordinates (`scripts/data/capitals-coords-wikidata.json`, `src/data/capitals.ts`)
come from Wikidata (properties P36 and P625). Wikidata's structured data is
available under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
https://www.wikidata.org · https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Selection of countries

The 197 countries are the 193 UN member states (https://www.un.org/en/about-us/member-states),
the two UN observer states (Vatican City and Palestine), Kosovo and Taiwan.
This is a list of facts and is not copied from any copyrighted source.

## Natural Earth – map images

The maps in `assets/maps/*.png` and the projection data in `src/data/maps.ts` were
generated by `scripts/gen-maps.ts` from Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 – Countries.
Natural Earth data is in the public domain. Made with Natural Earth.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com

Natural Earth shows de facto boundaries. Borders and the status of disputed
territories on the maps do not express any political position.
